PRIVACY POLICY

of the “Falling Buttons” Mobile Application

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. General Provisions

1.1. This document (hereinafter referred to as the “Policy”) defines the procedure for processing information collected during the use of the “Falling Buttons” mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the “Application”).

1.2. The Application is developed by Dmitry Vladimirovich Dremin (hereinafter referred to as the “Developer”).

1.3. By using the Application, the User agrees to the terms of this Policy. If the User does not agree with its terms, they must discontinue using the Application and uninstall it from their device.

1.4. The Developer does not independently collect or process Users' personal data. Information obtained during the operation of the Application may be processed in anonymized form using integrated third-party software development kits (SDKs).

2. Legal Grounds and Purposes of Information Processing

2.1. The legal basis for information processing is the User's consent, expressed through the installation and use of the Application, as well as other grounds provided for by applicable law.

2.2. Anonymized information is processed for the following purposes:

Ensuring stable, secure, and proper operation of the Application;

Displaying advertising materials;

Analyzing the use of game features and Merge mechanics;

Analyzing game sessions and User interactions with game elements;

Improving gameplay, the user interface, and Application performance;

Collecting usage statistics and diagnosing technical errors;

Preventing misuse and ensuring the proper functioning of services.

3. Types of Information Processed

3.1. The Developer does not directly collect, store, or process Users' personal data, unless otherwise stated.

3.2. The Application integrates third-party software libraries (SDKs) that may automatically collect technical information.

The following service is used at the time of publication:

Yandex Ads SDK — for displaying advertising materials.

3.3. The following information may be processed through the specified SDK:

Device technical specifications (device model, manufacturer, operating system version, memory capacity, and screen resolution);

Device advertising identifiers;

Data concerning interactions with the Application;

Game session statistics;

Information about crashes, errors, and performance;

IP address to the extent necessary for the operation of advertising and technical services.

3.4. As part of its gameplay functionality, the Application may use technical information about User actions, game events, combinations of game elements, and game session results to ensure proper gameplay and maintain the functionality of the Application.

4. Information Processing Procedures and Conditions

4.1. Information is processed using automated means.

4.2. Information collected by third-party SDKs may be transferred to and processed by the respective service providers in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4.3. The Developer does not sell or transfer information to third parties, except in the following cases:

Use of the third-party services specified in this Policy;

Compliance with the requirements of the laws of the Russian Federation.

4.4. The Application does not require Users to independently enter or provide the following information for its core gameplay functionality:

User's first name, last name, or patronymic;

Passport details or other identification documents;

Residential addresses;

Telephone numbers;

Email addresses;

Login credentials or passwords;

Precise geolocation data.

4.5. Users may review Yandex's Privacy Policy at:

https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/

5.1. The Application may display advertisements provided by third-party services.

5.2. Advertising identifiers and anonymized technical information may be used to display advertisements.

5.3. Users may restrict personalized advertising through their device settings, subject to the capabilities of their operating system.

6.1. Users may discontinue using the Application at any time.

6.2. Users may restrict the use of advertising identifiers through their device's operating system settings.

6.3. Uninstalling the Application stops further collection of information by the Application itself. However, the processing of information previously transferred to third-party services is governed by their respective policies and rules.

6.4. The Developer is unable to identify a specific User based on anonymized data, provided that such data does not contain information that makes personal identification possible.

7.1. The Developer takes reasonable organizational and technical measures to protect information within the scope of their responsibility.
